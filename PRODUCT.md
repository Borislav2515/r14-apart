# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Travelers, couples, families, and business guests looking for short-term accommodation in Vladikavkaz. They arrive via search (Yandex/Google), referrals, or direct links — often on mobile, often comparing a few options quickly. They need to trust the space, see real photos, understand location and amenities, and book without friction or middleman noise.

## Product Purpose

Present R14-APART — a premium boutique apartment at ul. Revolutsii 14 in historic central Vladikavkaz — and convert visitors into confirmed bookings through the HomeReserve widget. Secondary paths: WhatsApp/Telegram contact and photo exploration for guests who need reassurance before committing. Success is a completed reservation with confidence; the site should feel like stepping into the apartment, not browsing a listing aggregator.

## Brand Personality

Premium and welcoming. Modern boutique hospitality — calm, elegant, trustworthy, cozy. Locally rooted in Vladikavkaz character without resorting to tourist cliché. Voice is assured but warm: a thoughtful host, not a hotel chain or a bargain listing.

Three words: **refined · welcoming · local**

Emotional goals: calm confidence, effortless booking, the sense that this is a real place worth staying in.

## Anti-references

- OTA clutter — dense listing layouts, badge spam, price tables, comparison grids (Booking.com, Avito-style density)
- Generic hotel templates — stock photography, corporate blues, interchangeable "luxury" tropes
- AI landing page grammar — warm cream body backgrounds, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow kickers on every section, numbered section scaffolding
- Budget rental aesthetic — loud colors, cramped layouts, low-trust visual language
- Visual clutter that competes with photography and the booking widget

## Design Principles

1. **Photography carries the room** — large authentic images, generous whitespace; UI recedes so the apartment feels present.
2. **Book without friction** — the HomeReserve widget is the primary action; contact channels support, never overshadow, conversion.
3. **Boutique, not hotel** — editorial typography and restrained palette; local identity through detail and copy, not decorative regional motifs.
4. **Trust through clarity** — honest amenities, location, pricing context, and reviews; no hype or listing-aggregator tricks.
5. **Calm motion** — motion supports depth and reveal; never competes with content or gates visibility on animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** for text contrast, focus states, and interactive controls
- **Reduced motion** respected throughout — crossfade or instant alternatives for all animations (partially implemented)
- **Russian-first** — Cyrillic typography tested at mobile sizes; long headlines must not overflow at tablet breakpoints
- Skip link, semantic landmarks, and keyboard navigation maintained on all surfaces
